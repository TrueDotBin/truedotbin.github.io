### Hello, everyone!

You might've heard that I am rebranding to **TrueDotBin** (from my former name, **Demiomad**)

That's cool and all, but *why*?

### Demio

No, seriously, [Demio](https://demio.com) is a real thing. They seem to be offering some service.

Now, remember, I used to be known as Demiomad.

**Demio**mad... **Demio** mad...  
I think you can see what I mean.

I didn't want to have any name collisions and all that jazz so I've decided to rebrand

### Mispronounciations

Some people were not pronouncing Demiomad correctly (it was Deh-me-oh-mad), which was fine with me, but I don't want to cause any confusion. TrueDotBin is easier to pronounce (literally just true-dot-bin)

### Were You Thinking Of Other Names?

Yes! I was originally thinking of "TrueBinary", but that was taken.

"FalseBinary" was another option, but... that was also taken.

I thought about "FalsyBinary", since it's an actual term, but it's a one letter change and I felt like it would be stupid and kind of lazy.

I eventually landed on "true.bin" (or TrueDotBin), but most platforms don't let you use periods on usernames (I couldn't set my YouTube channel name to "true.bin" because it refused to accept that name.)
